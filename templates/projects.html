Games played through noise

Actions move the state, the state sets what others observe, and what they observe sets their next actions. My dissertation keeps that loop intact by tracking beliefs about the primitive shocks. Move a slider and the equilibrium is solved again, here, in about a second.

  • shock responses

A mesh that knows when to stop

Thousands of coins, each flipped a few times, and odds that change somewhere on the square. The estimator cuts a mesh only where a false-discovery gate says the flips justify it, then stops on its own. Draw the odds yourself and see what it can find.

  • both estimators, in WebAssembly
  • the gate, round by round
