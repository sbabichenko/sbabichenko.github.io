Interactive Demos

Noise-State Game Explorer

Actions move the state, the state sets what others observe, and what they observe sets their next actions. My dissertation keeps that loop intact by tracking beliefs about the primitive shocks. Move a slider and the equilibrium is solved again in about a second.

Solve a game →

Decision Mesh, Live

Thousands of coins, each flipped a few times, and odds that change somewhere on the square. The estimator adds detail only where a false-discovery gate says the flips justify it, then stops on its own. It separates the odds from each coin's own effect and from the noise of the flips. Draw the odds yourself and see what it can find.

Draw your own odds →
