Noise-State Calculus

My dissertation. Actions move the state, the state sets what others observe, and what they observe sets their next actions. I keep that loop intact by tracking beliefs about the primitive shocks. Move a slider and the equilibrium is solved again in about a second.

Decision Mesh

Thousands of coins, each flipped a few times, and odds that change somewhere on the square. The estimator adds detail only where a false-discovery gate says the flips justify it, then stops on its own. It separates the odds from each coin's own effect and from the noise of the flips.
